Pickleball 101

The rules, simply

  • Serve: underhand, diagonal, behind the baseline.
  • Two-bounce rule: the serve and return must each bounce once.
  • Kitchen: no volleying while standing in the 7-foot zone by the net.
  • Scoring: only the serving side scores. First to 11, win by 2.
  • Lines: a ball touching any line is in.

Questions

Quick answers

Do I need to know how to play?

Not at all. Join a Beginner session and we'll teach you everything.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. We lend paddles and balls. Many members buy their own after a few games.

How do I join a session?

Open the Schedule page, pick a session and hit Reserve. We'll confirm with you.

How much does it cost?

Each session shows its fee on the Schedule page, so you always know before you book.

Can kids and seniors play?

Yes! Pickleball is for all ages. Younger players should come with an adult.

What if it rains?

We post any changes on the News page and our social media.
