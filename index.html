Pickleball 101

Know the court

  • Serve underhand and diagonally across the court.
  • Two-bounce rule: let it bounce once on each side before volleying.
  • Stay out of the kitchen (the 7-foot zone by the net) when volleying.
  • Games go to 11. Win by 2 points.
